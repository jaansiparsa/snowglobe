# Snow Globe of Anything

CS184/284A (Fall 2026) final project — **project proposal**.

A real-time, interactive snow globe in the browser: drop a photo, a sketch, or a
Gaussian-splat capture inside a glass dome, then shake it with your mouse or your
phone and watch the snow swirl through refracted water. The project focuses on
three graphics problems working together in real time:

- **Glass refraction** — refraction through the curved dome and water surface, with Fresnel reflections.
- **Snow particle physics** — a GPU particle system that settles under gravity and is stirred on shake.
- **Swirling water** — a curl-noise (stretch goal: true fluid-solver) velocity field coupled to the snow.
